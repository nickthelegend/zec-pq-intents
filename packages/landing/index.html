zec-pq-intents

Intents a quantum computer can’t forge or read in transit.

Signed with ML-DSA-65, sealed to the solver with ML-KEM-768, settled in shielded ZEC on testnet. Every decision is a signed, hash-chained log entry anchored in a shielded memo, and anyone can re-verify all of it.

What is post-quantum here, and what is not

PiecePrimitive
who asked for what (intent, fill claim, log entries)ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204)
the intent in transit to the solverML-KEM-768 (FIPS 203) + AES-256-GCM
the moneyZcash shielded transactions (Ironwood) — elliptic-curve, not post-quantum

Limits

The verifier holds the escrow, so it is trusted to pay out; it cannot rewrite the record without the signatures, links or anchors showing it. ZEC-only delivery intents on testnet. Not audited.
