zec-pq-intentsGitHub

Intents signed and sealed with NIST post-quantum primitives.

An intent is signed with ML-DSA-65 and sealed to the solver with ML-KEM-768. The user escrows real testnet ZEC, the solver delivers from its own liquidity, and a verifier releases or refunds the escrow, writing every decision into a signed, hash-chained log anchored on-chain.

Three intents, one shielded funding transaction

        
Escrow 60,000 zat each, deliver 40,000. A release or a refund pays 60,000 minus a 15,000 fee budget, so 45,000 zat. Every payout was re-read with the recipient's viewing key.

What this is not

        
    
          
  • Not trustless: the verifier holds the escrow. The signed, anchored log makes every decision checkable after the fact.
    • 
          
  • Zcash spends stay elliptic-curve based. This is post-quantum at the intent layer only.
    • 
          
  • Testnet only, with demo wallets whose viewing keys are published. Not audited.
