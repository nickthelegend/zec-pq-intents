The intent layer uses NIST's post-quantum primitives, the ones Zcash's roadmap brings in later. Zcash spends themselves stay elliptic-curve based.

The user escrows real testnet ZEC. The solver delivers from its own liquidity, and a verifier releases the escrow or refunds it.

Frequently asked questions

What the promise covers, and what it doesn't.

Is it trustless?

No. The verifier holds the escrow and is trusted for liveness: it could withhold a payout. It cannot fake the record without the signatures, links or anchors showing it.

Is the ZEC payment post-quantum?

No. Zcash spends stay elliptic-curve based. Yaksok is post-quantum at the intent layer only.

Who can read my intent?

The solver, once it opens the envelope. On a quiet testnet, amounts and timing can still link a funding and a fill.

Is it live?

On Zcash testnet, ZEC only, with no cross-asset leg and a single verifier key. Not audited. A portfolio piece; the Zecathon entry is Kage (Shielded x402).
